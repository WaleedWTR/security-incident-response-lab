# Incident Triage Playbook

## Minimum triage record

- incident identifier
- reporter/detection source
- first observed time
- affected user/device/service
- observable evidence
- preliminary business impact
- initial severity
- owner
- next action

## Triage sequence

1. validate that the signal is real
2. assess urgency and impact
3. preserve volatile evidence where required
4. identify affected entities
5. search for related activity
6. decide whether containment is justified
7. record assumptions separately from facts
