# Security Incident Communications

## Principles

Communications should be:

- factual
- time-stamped
- audience-appropriate
- explicit about what is known versus under investigation
- clear about actions and next update

## Example update structure

- incident status
- affected services/users
- business impact
- actions completed
- actions in progress
- risks / unknowns
- next update time

Avoid including unnecessary technical indicators or personal data in broad stakeholder updates.
